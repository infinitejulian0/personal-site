Account & data deletion

Effective September 25, 2026

You can delete your Lucid account in the app. If you cannot access it, we can help at infinite@julian.ai.

Delete in Lucid

  1. Open Lucid and go to Settings.
  2. Choose Delete account in the Account section.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm.
  4. If your account is linked to Apple, complete Apple's confirmation to revoke access. Cancellation or a revocation failure stops the deletion.

Save any notes you want to keep before deleting. Signing out, removing the app, or revoking Google/Apple access outside Lucid does not by itself delete your Lucid account.

What this removes

In-app deletion removes your Supabase authentication account, its synced library records, and this device's account library and pending sync outbox. This includes account-owned videos, notes, conversations, citations, and revisions in that library.

Deletion automatically queues removal of associated Worker diagnostics, recordings, temporary enhancement jobs and account-linked PostHog events and recordings. You do not need to make a second request. Cleanup retries temporary failures and checks provider completion. Allow up to 30 days for associated data and backup expiry. Minimal deletion receipts and irreversible installation hashes may remain to prevent delayed uploads from recreating deleted records.

This device’s recordings, diagnostics and consent choices are also removed. Interrupted local cleanup resumes when Lucid next opens. Provider security, abuse-prevention and legal retention can differ; see the privacy policy.

Delete without the app or get help

Email infinite@julian.ai with the subject Lucid data deletion. Tell us:

  • The email address shown in Lucid, including the Apple private relay address if applicable.
  • Whether you want the whole account deleted or a specific data request.
  • Whether you already used in-app deletion, and approximately when.

We may ask you to verify ownership before deleting data. Never send your password, sign-in code, or unnecessary copies of private content. We will confirm the outcome, explain any data we must retain, and respond within the period required by applicable law. You may also request access, correction, or export through this address.

Other devices and providers

Devices can hold offline copies until they reconnect or their local app data is removed. Sign out and remove Lucid from devices you no longer use. Account deletion does not delete your Apple, Google, or YouTube account, and those providers may retain their own security records under their policies.
