Lucid helps you learn from videos through conversation and notes. This policy explains what information the Lucid iPhone beta handles, why it is used, and how you can ask us to delete it.

Mindflow Inc. is the publisher of Lucid, also listed as Lucid Video Companion in TestFlight. Our contact is Julian Alvarez at infinite@julian.ai. This policy covers Lucid and these Lucid support pages, not unrelated products on julian.ai.

Information we collect

  • Account and profile. When you sign in with Apple or Google, Supabase, our authentication provider, stores your account identifier, email address, provider identity, and profile information supplied by that provider. Google can supply your name and profile-image URL. Apple can supply a private relay email. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.
  • Your library. Videos and their links, playback progress, notes, conversation text, citations, and note revisions are saved on your device and synced to your account. Questions and saved tool-call history can include web-search queries and links.
  • Voice and AI requests. When voice is active, microphone audio is sent to our voice services. Questions, conversation history, relevant video transcripts, search queries, and note content are sent to AI services as needed to answer you, control playback, or create and improve notes. Nearby sounds or voices may be captured by your microphone. AI responses can be inaccurate.
  • Technical diagnostics. We collect installation identifiers, app and device details, technical events, crash and hang summaries, timing, and other performance measurements. Encrypted diagnostic snapshots can also include conversation text, notes, video links, citations, and playback events. Ordinary diagnostic snapshots do not contain raw microphone recordings.
  • Usage analytics and interface replay. PostHog receives feature-use events, app/device properties, and a hashed account identifier. Analytics begins before sign-in using an anonymous identifier. Hashing an account identifier does not make it anonymous. The beta also uses interface session replay configured to mask text inputs, images, and embedded web views. Interface replay is not a recording of microphone or assistant audio.
  • Optional diagnostic recordings. Authorized diagnostic users can start a recording after a separate disclosure. The recording, related events, and context are uploaded encrypted and analyzed automatically when recording stops. It can contain your voice and nearby sounds. This feature is separate from normal voice conversations.
  • Support and service logs. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send. Hosting, authentication, and other service providers also process network information, such as IP addresses and request metadata, to deliver and secure their services.

Why we use information

We use this information to authenticate your account, sync your library, provide video and AI features, process support requests, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, and understand and improve the beta. We do not sell your personal information or use Lucid's account and product-analytics data for cross-company targeted advertising.

Do not include passwords or information you do not want processed by these services in conversations, notes, or support reports. This policy is a notice; it does not replace any permission the app must request from you.

Services that process information

  • Supabase stores authentication information and your synced library.
  • Cloudflare runs backend services and stores diagnostics and temporary processing results.
  • LiveKit carries voice-session audio and connection metadata.
  • Google Gemini processes voice or text requests, relevant conversation and video context, note requests, and web searches. Supadata obtains video transcripts.
  • PostHog processes product analytics and interface replay.
  • Alternative diagnostic/admin voice modes can use OpenAI, AssemblyAI, ElevenLabs, or Deepgram for model, speech-recognition, or speech-output processing. The information sent depends on the selected mode.

Service providers receive information needed for their role and are subject to their applicable service terms, data-protection commitments, and security obligations. We require providers processing information for us to protect it consistently with this policy. Provider retention for security, abuse prevention, backups, or legal obligations can differ from the storage periods below. We do not promise that all providers immediately delete requests or that all processing has zero retention.

Lucid uses the paid Gemini API. Under Google’s paid-service terms, prompts and responses are not used to improve Google products. Google can retain them for a limited period for safety, abuse prevention, and required legal disclosures. This is not a promise of zero retention. Avoid sharing sensitive or confidential information you do not want processed by these services.

Provider information: Supabase, Cloudflare, LiveKit, Gemini API data terms, Supadata, PostHog, OpenAI, AssemblyAI, ElevenLabs, and Deepgram.

YouTube and external links

Lucid uses YouTube's embedded player and related YouTube services. YouTube supplies the video and may serve advertisements and collect information directly, including through cookies or similar technologies in the player. That processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service. Opening an external source also shares ordinary network information with that site. Lucid does not require your YouTube password or request access to your private YouTube account history.

You can review and revoke Google account connections in Google account settings. Revoking a provider connection is separate from deleting information already stored by Lucid.

How long information is kept

  • Account and library: retained while your account exists, unless you delete individual records or your account. Sign-out preserves local data for that account; it is not deletion.
  • Worker diagnostics and content snapshots: expire after 30 days. A new upload can refresh a snapshot's expiry. Scheduled cleanup removes expired records.
  • Temporary note-enhancement results: expire after 24 hours.
  • Uploaded diagnostic audio: expires after 72 hours remotely. Local recordings remain until deleted.
  • PostHog interface replay: the current project is configured for 30-day retention. Separately saved exports, if made for a reported problem, are handled with that support investigation.
  • Product analytics and support: analytics events do not currently have an application-enforced automatic expiry. We retain them to evaluate beta use and investigate problems, review their need when the beta ends, and handle verified deletion requests through the contact below. Support correspondence is retained for the request and any necessary follow-up.

Backups and security or legal records may remain for the period needed for recovery, security, or a legal obligation. If we must retain information after a deletion request, we will explain the applicable reason when responding.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete library items in Lucid. To delete your account, open Settings → Delete account, type DELETE, and complete the confirmation. Apple-linked accounts also confirm revocation with Apple. This removes the Supabase account and library rows and this device's account library and sync outbox.

Account deletion does not immediately erase previously collected Worker diagnostics, temporary enhancement results, or PostHog analytics. To request deletion of those records or to delete an account when you cannot use the app, email infinite@julian.ai. See account and data deletion for what to include. We may verify ownership before acting. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, or private recordings.

Turn off microphone access in iOS Settings to prevent new microphone capture. Close the video to end its voice session. To withdraw permission for optional processing or request access, correction, export, restriction, or deletion of personal information, contact us. The current beta does not provide a separate in-app switch for PostHog analytics; stopping use prevents new app activity while we handle your request. Depending on where you live, you may also have a right to object or complain to your local data-protection authority. We respond within the time required by applicable law.

Security and processing locations

We use encrypted network connections, account-based access controls, and encryption for stored diagnostic content. No service can guarantee absolute security. Services may process information in the United States and other countries where their infrastructure operates. Applicable provider agreements and transfer safeguards govern those transfers.

Age and changes to this policy

The Lucid beta is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it as appropriate.

We update this page when our practices change, revise the effective date, and provide additional notice or request permission when required. For questions, contact infinite@julian.ai.
