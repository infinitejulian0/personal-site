Lucid helps you learn from videos through conversation and notes. This policy explains what information the Lucid iPhone beta handles, why it is used, and how you can ask us to delete it.

Mindflow Inc. is the publisher of Lucid, also listed as Lucid - Talk to any Video in TestFlight. Our contact is Julian Alvarez at infinite@julian.ai. This policy covers Lucid and these Lucid support pages, not unrelated products on julian.ai.

Information we collect

  • Account and profile. When you sign in with Apple or Google, Supabase, our authentication provider, stores your account identifier, email address, provider identity, and profile information supplied by that provider. Google can supply your name and profile-image URL. Apple can supply a private relay email. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.
  • Your library. Videos and their links, playback progress, notes, conversation text, citations, and note revisions are saved on your device and synced to your account. Questions and saved tool-call history can include web-search queries and links.
  • Voice and AI requests. AI processing starts after you accept the linked terms through the sign-in action. AI powers Lucid; opening a video connects voice and prepares video context; while voice is active, microphone audio is sent to our voice services. Questions, conversation history, relevant video transcripts, search queries, and note content are sent to AI services as needed to answer you, control playback, or create and improve notes. Nearby sounds or voices may be captured by your microphone. AI responses can be inaccurate.
  • Technical diagnostics. We collect installation identifiers, app and device details, technical events, crash and hang summaries, timing, and other performance measurements. Automatic content-bearing diagnostic snapshots and learning analytics are disabled in the external beta. Essential diagnostics do not contain raw microphone recordings. Earlier internal builds may have uploaded encrypted conversation and note snapshots, which remain subject to deletion and expiry.
  • Optional usage analytics. Only after you enable usage analytics, PostHog receives feature-use events, app/device properties, and a hashed account identifier. It does not start before your choice. Hashing an account identifier does not make it anonymous. Interface session replay is disabled in the external beta.
  • Optional diagnostic recordings. Authorized diagnostic users can start a recording after a separate disclosure. The recording, related events, and context are uploaded encrypted and analyzed automatically when recording stops. It can contain your voice and nearby sounds. This feature is separate from normal voice conversations.
  • Support and service logs. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send. Hosting, authentication, and other service providers also process network information, such as IP addresses and request metadata, to deliver and secure their services.

Why we use information

We use this information to authenticate your account, sync your library, provide video and AI features, process support requests, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, and understand and improve the beta. We do not sell your personal information or use Lucid's account and product-analytics data for cross-company targeted advertising.

Do not include passwords or information you do not want processed by these services in conversations, notes, or support reports. This policy is a notice; it does not replace any permission the app must request from you.

Services that process information

  • Supabase stores authentication information and your synced library.
  • Cloudflare runs backend services and stores diagnostics and temporary processing results.
  • LiveKit carries voice-session audio and connection metadata.
  • Google Gemini processes voice or text requests, relevant conversation and video context, note requests, and web searches. Video understanding uses Google’s documented public YouTube URL input. Lucid does not scrape video captions or download videos for playback.
  • Supadata receives the public video link to fetch captions for transcript processing. Google Gemini is used when captions are unavailable.
  • PostHog processes optional product analytics.
  • Depending on the voice mode and available fallbacks, OpenAI, AssemblyAI, ElevenLabs, Cartesia, or Deepgram also provide model, speech-recognition, or speech-output processing. The information sent depends on the selected mode.

Service providers receive information needed for their role and are subject to their applicable service terms, data-protection commitments, and security obligations. We require providers processing information for us to protect it consistently with this policy. Provider retention for security, abuse prevention, backups, or legal obligations can differ from the storage periods below. We do not promise that all providers immediately delete requests or that all processing has zero retention.

Lucid uses the paid Gemini API. Under Google’s paid-service terms, prompts and responses are not used to improve Google products. Google can retain them for a limited period for safety, abuse prevention, and required legal disclosures. This is not a promise of zero retention. Avoid sharing sensitive or confidential information you do not want processed by these services.

Provider information: Supabase, Cloudflare, LiveKit, Gemini API data terms, PostHog, OpenAI, AssemblyAI, ElevenLabs, Cartesia, and Deepgram.

YouTube and external links

Lucid uses YouTube’s embedded player with temporary website storage. Opening a video loads YouTube services and may start playback automatically. Playback can continue in the background or Picture in Picture. These features continue sending playback requests to YouTube. YouTube supplies the video and may serve advertisements and collect information directly, including through cookies or similar technologies in the player. This can include approximate location derived from your IP address, device identifiers, video interactions and ad impressions for playback, security, advertising and measurement. That processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service. Opening an external source also shares ordinary network information with that site. Lucid does not require your YouTube password or request access to your private YouTube account history.

You can review and revoke Google account connections in Google account settings. Revoking a provider connection is separate from deleting information already stored by Lucid.

How long information is kept

  • Account and library: retained while your account exists, unless you delete individual records or your account. Sign-out preserves local data for that account; it is not deletion.
  • Worker diagnostics and content snapshots: expire after 30 days. A new upload can refresh a snapshot's expiry. Scheduled cleanup removes expired records.
  • Temporary note-enhancement results: expire after 24 hours.
  • Uploaded diagnostic audio: expires after 72 hours remotely. Local recordings remain until deleted.
  • Earlier internal interface replay: existing recordings are configured for 30-day retention. New interface replay is disabled. Account deletion also requests removal of existing recordings.
  • Product analytics and support: analytics events do not currently have an application-enforced automatic expiry. We retain them to evaluate beta use and investigate problems, review their need when the beta ends, and automatically request removal of account-linked events when you delete your account. Support correspondence is retained for the request and any necessary follow-up.

Backups and security or legal records may remain for the period needed for recovery, security, or a legal obligation. If we must retain information after a deletion request, we will explain the applicable reason when responding.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete library items in Lucid. To delete your account, open Settings → Delete account, type DELETE, and complete the confirmation. Apple-linked accounts also confirm revocation with Apple. This removes the Supabase account and library rows and this device’s account library, recordings, diagnostics, consent choices and sync outbox. Server-side cleanup of associated diagnostics, recordings, processing jobs and PostHog data is then queued automatically and retried through temporary service failures.

You do not need to email us separately to remove associated app data. Provider cleanup is asynchronous; we check completion and repeat cleanup to cover requests already in flight. Allow up to 30 days for associated data and backup expiry. Minimal deletion receipts and irreversible installation hashes can remain to prevent delayed uploads recreating erased records. Provider security, abuse-prevention or legal records may remain under their own policies. See account and data deletion.

The sign-in screen links the terms, which include the AI processing details and named providers. By continuing with that action, you accept those terms; successful sign-in opens Lucid directly, without a separate consent page. A restored account without permission stays on the same sign-in screen until you choose Continue. AI is part of using Lucid, rather than a separate mode. Signing out withdraws permission on that device and stops new AI requests; the next sign-in accepts the displayed agreement again. Account deletion remains available before and after acceptance. Withdrawal cannot undo processing already sent. The small Privacy link at the bottom of Settings opens the policy and optional usage analytics, which stays off unless you choose to share it. Turning analytics off stops the SDK and clears its local state. Earlier beta builds may show a welcome agreement or separate privacy controls. Essential security and service diagnostics remain enabled. You can also turn off microphone access in iOS Settings and close a video to end its voice session.

For access, correction, export, restriction, deletion when you cannot use the app, or a privacy concern, contact infinite@julian.ai. We may verify ownership. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes. Depending on where you live, you may also have a right to object or complain to your local data-protection authority. We respond within the time required by applicable law.

Security and processing locations

We use encrypted network connections, account-based access controls, and encryption for stored diagnostic content. No service can guarantee absolute security. Services may process information in the United States and other countries where their infrastructure operates. Applicable provider agreements and transfer safeguards govern those transfers.

Age and changes to this policy

The Lucid beta is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it as appropriate.

We update this page when our practices change, revise the effective date, and provide additional notice or request permission when required. For questions, contact infinite@julian.ai.
