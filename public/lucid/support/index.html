Lucid Video Companion is an early iPhone beta from Mindflow Inc. It lets you ask questions about YouTube videos and save notes alongside them. It requires iOS 18.2 or later, an Apple or Google account, and an internet connection for playback, voice, and sync. Beta availability is controlled through TestFlight.

Report a problem

Use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight or email us. Helpful details include:

  • What you were doing, what you expected, and what happened.
  • Your iPhone model, iOS version, and Lucid build number shown in TestFlight.
  • The approximate time and whether you were using headphones, Bluetooth, or the speaker.
  • A video link or screenshot, only if you are comfortable sharing it.

Please do not send passwords, verification codes, private notes, or recordings unless you deliberately choose to share that material. We will never ask for your Apple or Google password.

Sign-in and missing notes

Sign in with the same account you used previously. Apple's Hide My Email can use a different address from Google. Check your connection and allow the library to sync before changing accounts. Contact us before deleting or reinstalling the app if you have unsynced notes.

Voice and video

Check microphone access in iOS Settings and confirm your audio route. An internet connection is required. Try reopening the video if the connection stalls. Some videos do not have usable transcripts. AI can make mistakes; verify important information against the original source.

Privacy and account help

You can delete your account in Lucid Settings → Delete account. For the steps, what is removed, and help when you cannot sign in, see account and data deletion. Our privacy policy explains voice processing, diagnostics, analytics, and retention.

Using the beta

This beta is intended for adults 18 and over. Features may change or be unavailable. It is not an emergency service or a source of professional advice. Use it only where it is safe to interact with your phone. See the beta terms.
